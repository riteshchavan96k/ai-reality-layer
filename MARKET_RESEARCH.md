# AI Reality Layer — Market Research

## 1. Research Objective

Determine whether there is a sufficiently valuable problem around trustworthy, current, evidence-backed real-world context for AI applications and agents.

## 2. Core Market Hypothesis

AI applications increasingly need reliable access to changing external and organizational information.

As AI agents become more capable and autonomous, incorrect, stale, incomplete, conflicting, or poorly sourced information can create significant failures.

## 3. Initial Target Market for Investigation

AI application developers and teams building AI agents.

This is a research focus, not yet the final customer segment.

## 4. Core Problem Under Investigation

AI systems may need to reason about real-world information that:

- changes over time
- comes from multiple sources
- contains contradictions
- lacks clear provenance
- may become stale
- has different levels of source reliability
- contains uncertainty

## 5. Current Solution Categories

Potentially competing or complementary categories include:

- web search
- retrieval systems
- RAG systems
- enterprise search
- AI grounding
- AI observability
- AI evaluation
- agent orchestration
- AI security and governance

## 6. Potential Differentiation Hypothesis

AI Reality Layer may focus on representing real-world information as structured, evidence-backed reality states rather than treating retrieved documents or model outputs as the final representation of reality.

Potential components include:

- claims
- evidence
- provenance
- freshness
- conflicts
- confidence
- temporal state

This differentiation is a hypothesis and must be validated.

## 7. Candidate Use Cases

### A. Enterprise research agents

Agents require current and evidence-backed information for research.

### B. Procurement agents

Agents verify supplier status, policies, approvals, and other changing information.

### C. Customer-support agents

Agents need current company policies and product information.

### D. Business intelligence agents

Agents need current company, market, operational, and financial information.

### E. Research agents

Agents need to track claims, sources, contradictions, and changes over time.

## 8. Evaluation Criteria

Candidate use cases should be evaluated using:

1. Pain
2. Frequency
3. Financial value
4. Existing alternatives
5. Technical feasibility
6. User accessibility
7. Urgency
8. Differentiation
9. Data availability
10. Ability to build with limited capital

## 9. Current Evidence

Research indicates that:

- AI agents are increasingly being deployed in production.
- Reliability and quality remain major challenges.
- Real-time context is increasingly important for agentic systems.
- AI observability and evaluation have become established infrastructure categories.
- Enterprise AI systems increasingly depend on trusted business context.

## 10. Competitive Insight

Existing infrastructure already addresses significant parts of the broader problem.

Therefore AI Reality Layer must not simply become:

- another chatbot
- another search API
- another generic RAG framework
- another observability dashboard
- another evaluation platform

The product must identify a specific unresolved problem and provide a differentiated solution.

## 11. Key Strategic Question

Why should an AI application use an AI Reality Layer instead of directly using:

- search
- RAG
- enterprise search
- existing grounding systems
- observability/evaluation platforms
- direct database/API access

This question must be answered with evidence before major product development.

## 12. Current Conclusion

The broad problem appears credible and strategically relevant, but the specific commercial opportunity has not yet been proven.

The next research objective is to identify the highest-value initial user and use case.

## 13. Status

Research in progress.
